Privacy Policy

Ridgeline MX (ridgeline-mx.com) · Last updated: November 8, 2026

This page explains what information the Ridgeline MX game collects, why, and what choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Who we are

Ridgeline MX is an independent online off-road racing game. Questions, or want something deleted? Email us at CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE.

What we collect

  • Account details: the rider name you choose and your password. Your password is never stored as text: only a salted, one-way hash is kept.
  • Game progress: coins, items you own, stats, level, ranked rating, friends list, and your settings.
  • Chat messages: messages you send in the game chat. They are cleared automatically after a few hours.
  • Device and network information: a random device ID kept in your browser, and your IP address. We use these only to stop cheating, ban evasion and creating many accounts.
  • Gameplay data in multiplayer: your position and actions in a race, sent to the other players in your room so the race works.

We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number or date of birth, and we do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and local storage

The game stores small pieces of data in your browser (local storage, and a cookie for the device ID) to keep you logged in, remember your settings and save your progress. These are needed for the game to work. If you clear your browser data you may lose your login and offline progress.

Advertising

We may show ads, for example a video you can choose to watch for bonus coins. Ads are provided by third-party advertising partners. They may use cookies or similar identifiers to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK) we only load advertising cookies after you accept them in the cookie notice, and you can change your choice by clearing this site's data and reloading. Each ad partner has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep things

Account data is kept while your account exists. Chat messages are removed after a few hours. Deleted extra accounts are held for up to 14 days and then removed. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Children

Ridgeline MX is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child gave us information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us. You can also stop us collecting new data by not using the game.

Security

Passwords are hashed, and the game is served over HTTPS. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Big changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
